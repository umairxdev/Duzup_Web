Privacy

Your reminders stay yours.

Duzup has no INTERNET permission. It cannot send your data anywhere because it cannot connect to anything. There is no account, no analytics and no ads. Backups are plain files you choose where to save.

Permissions it asks for

  • NotificationsTo show your reminders. Asked for in context, never at launch.
  • Exact alarmsOptional. For on-the-minute timing. Falls back gracefully without it.
  • Run at startupTo bring your reminders back after a reboot.

Exported backups are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Duzup?

Duzup is a free, open-source reminder app for Android. It keeps bills, events, tasks, notes, birthdays and routines in one place and sends heads-up notifications at the right time. It works fully offline, with no account, no ads and no tracking.

Is Duzup free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I install it?

Download the APK from the button above, open it on your phone, and allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks. Then open Duzup and allow notifications when prompted.

Will my reminders survive a reboot?

Yes. Duzup restores its reminders after a reboot, an app update and when the time or time zone changes.

A reminder came late. Why?

Some phone makers restrict background apps aggressively. In Settings, Duzup can open your battery settings so you can let it run unrestricted. If exact alarms are off, reminders may land a few minutes late.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings, export a backup file, move it to the new phone and import it. Without a backup, uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes your items.
